About Me

I have always been someone who enjoys learning, working with others, and taking on new challenges. My experiences as a student and Division I student athlete have shaped the way I approach both my education and my future career. Being part of a team for most of my life taught me the importance of communication, collaboration, time management, and being willing to learn from the people around me.

I graduated from East Carolina University with a Bachelor of Science in Communication. My time at ECU allowed me to explore my interests in communication, sports, leadership, and working with different organizations. I am now continuing my education at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in the Master of Arts in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship program. Through MEITE, I am expanding my interests into education, technology, innovation, and how new ideas can create better experiences for learners and organizations.

Along with my graduate studies at UNC, I am currently interning with Innovate Carolina at Launch Chapel Hill. This experience has given me the opportunity to see entrepreneurship and innovation outside of the classroom and work on projects that support real companies and entrepreneurs. My internship allows me to combine communication, storytelling, research, and creative thinking while learning more about how organizations grow. Together, my coursework and internship are helping me explore where my interests in communication, innovation, education, and entrepreneurship can take me next.
